Bible Vibes – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what the Android and iOS app "Bible Vibes" (package name com.biblewallpaper.biblevibes) collects and why. The developer is Cheung Ngo Ming. Contact: cs.biblevibes@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. The app signs you in without an email or password, using an anonymous ID from Firebase Authentication. We use it to save your settings and to link your Premium subscription to your app.
  • Purchase and subscription records. If you buy Premium, Google Play processes the payment. We keep the purchase record (product, status, dates, purchase token) to unlock Premium. We never see your card details.
  • Usage analytics. We use Firebase Analytics to see which features are used. This runs only if you agree in the app.
  • Crash and error reports. We use Sentry to receive crash reports (for example device model, system version, app version and the error). We use them to fix bugs.
  • Device identifiers. Firebase creates an installation ID for the app on your device.

What stays on your device

Your chosen verses, wallpapers, widget settings and reminder times are stored on your device. Reminders are scheduled on your device. The app asks for notification permission so it can show them.

Ads

The app does not show ads at this time. If we add ads in the future, we will update this policy first.

Who we share data with

We use Google (Firebase and Google Play) and Sentry to run the app. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep data and how to delete it

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes your anonymous account and the data linked to it on our servers. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). More steps: Delete your account.

Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
