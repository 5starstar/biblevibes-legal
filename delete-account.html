Delete your Bible Vibes account

App: Bible Vibes (developer Cheung Ngo Ming). Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Bible Vibes.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This removes your anonymous account and the data linked to it on our servers, including your subscription record. Settings stored only on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.

Important: cancel your subscription yourself

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription, and you will keep being billed until you cancel. To cancel: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bible Vibes → Cancel. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

If you cannot open the app

Your account has no email address, so we cannot find it from your email alone. Email cs.biblevibes@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and include your Google Play order number (from your purchase receipt email) if you bought Premium. We will delete the records linked to that order and reply to confirm.
